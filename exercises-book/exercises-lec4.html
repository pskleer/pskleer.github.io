Consider a knapsack problem in which we have k = n^2 \in \mathbb{N} items I = \{1,\dots,k\} that all have a weight w_i and a value v_i for i \in I. We want to select a subset of items whose total weight is at most W, whilst their total value is maximized at the same time. Furthermore, we can select at most r items from the first group of n items \{1,\dots,n\}, at most r from the second group of n items \{n+1,\dots,2n\} etc.

This problem can be modelled with the following binary linear optimization problem, where x_i = 1 if item i is included, and x_i = 0 otherwise.

\begin{array}{lllll} \max & \displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^{n^2} v_ix_i & & \\ \rm{s.t. } & \displaystyle \sum_{i=a\cdot n+1}^{(a+1)\cdot n} x_{i} & \leq & r & \text{for } a = 0,\dots,n-1 \\ & \displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^{n^2} w_ix_{i} & \leq & W & \\ & x_{i} & \in & \{0,1\} & \text{for all } i = 1,\dots,n^2 \end{array}

Question 5

A university wants to assign bachelor students to thesis topics. Each teacher proposes exactly one topic, and each topic is proposed by exactly one teacher. Assigning a student to a topic therefore also determines their supervisor.

There are m students and n teachers. Every student has given every teacher’s topic a grade between 1 and 10, where a higher grade indicates a stronger preference. These grades are stored in a matrix G \in [1,10]^{m \times n}, where G_{ij} is the grade given by student i to the topic proposed by teacher j.

The university wants to maximize the total preference score, subject to the following constraints:

  • Every student must be assigned to exactly one teacher.
  • Each teacher can supervise at most U = 5 students.
  • For any two teachers, the numbers of students they supervise may differ by at most 2.

We use student indices i = 0,\dots,m-1 and teacher indices j = 0,\dots,n-1. Define binary decision variables x_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if student } i \text{ is assigned to teacher } j,\\ 0 & \text{otherwise}. \end{cases}

The problem can be formulated as the following binary linear optimization problem: \begin{array}{rll} \max & \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}G_{ij}x_{ij} & \text{total preference score}\\[6pt] \text{s.t.} & \displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}x_{ij}=1 & \forall i=0,\dots,m-1,\\[6pt] & \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_{ij}\leq U & \forall j=0,\dots,n-1,\\[6pt] & \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_{ip} -\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_{iq}\leq2 & \forall\,0\leq p<q<n,\\[6pt] & \displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_{iq} -\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_{ip}\leq2 & \forall\,0\leq p<q<n,\\[6pt] & x_{ij}\in\{0,1\} & \forall i,j. \end{array}

The two constraints for each pair of teachers ensure that their supervision loads differ by at most 2 in either direction. Teachers who supervise no students are included in this comparison.

  1. Write a function thesis_assignment() that takes as input a two-dimensional array G containing the preference grades and the maximum number of students per teacher, U. Return a Model() object implementing the problem. Do not optimize the model inside the function.
    Hint: You can store each teacher’s supervision load as a quicksum() expression and reuse these expressions in the balancing constraints.
